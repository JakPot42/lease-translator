How it works
1. Claude Extracts
Lease text → structured schema (notice periods, fees, deposit terms). You confirm before anything else runs.
2. Z3 Verifies
The Z3 SMT solver checks for logical contradictions — clauses that mathematically cannot both be true.
3. Claude Explains
Any contradiction is explained in plain language — what both clauses say, why they conflict, and what it means for you.
